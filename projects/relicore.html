Relicore

A GeoGuessr-inspired game: instead of finding your location, you're shown a historical artifact and guess where it came from.

Year

Role
Design & build
Stack

Each round shows an artifact from a world museum. Drop a pin on the map where you think it's from — the closer the guess, the higher the score.

Features

  • 460+ artifacts from the Metropolitan Museum, Cleveland Museum, Victoria & Albert Museum and Smithsonian.
  • Two modes: Normal (guess the location) and Timeline (location and year, with an era tolerance).
  • Interactive world map with pin placement, powered by MapLibre GL.
  • Distance-based scoring with era bonuses and streak multipliers.
  • Categories: All Eras, Ancient, Medieval, Modern, Asian Art, European Art.
